Quick Capture Setup

Send text straight to Comms Queue in 2 taps — from Slack, Mail, anywhere.

iPhone — iOS Shortcut

1
Open the Shortcuts app and tap + to create a new shortcut.
2
Tap Add Action, search for "Get Clipboard", and add it.
3
Add a second action: search "Open URL". Enter this URL:



Then tap the variable token at the end and choose Clipboard.
4
Rename the shortcut "Send to Queue" and tap Done.
5
To use: Copy any text in Slack or Mail, tap Share, find Send to Queue, and tap it.

Desktop — Bookmarklet

1
Show your bookmarks bar with Cmd+Shift+B in Chrome or Safari.
2
Drag the button below directly into your bookmarks bar.
Drag to bookmarks bar
Send to Queue
Copy any text in Slack or Gmail, then click the bookmark. Comms Queue opens with the text pre-filled and ready to triage.

Chrome: Click Allow if prompted about clipboard access.

Google Calendar Integration

1
One-tap add (per task): Any task with a due date shows a small GCal button in the list view, and an Add to Google Calendar button in the day detail panel. Tap it and Google Calendar opens with the event pre-filled — title, date, bucket, ministry tag, and source. Just hit Save.
2
Subscribe (auto-sync): Subscribe to the live feed URL below in Google Calendar once and all due-dated tasks appear automatically — no tapping required per task.

Calendar View Tips

→
Tasks without a due date appear as chips at the top of the calendar. Drag any chip onto a day to set its due date — it auto-assigns to This Week or Next Week based on the date.
→
Tap any day with tasks to see the full detail panel for that day.
