Setup

Get Comms Queue on your home screen and set up quick capture.

Add to Home Screen

1
Open this app in Safari — it must be Safari, not Chrome or another browser.
2
Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen — the box with an arrow pointing up.
3
Scroll down in the share sheet and tap Add to Home Screen.
4
Tap Add in the top right. The Comms Queue icon will appear on your home screen and open full screen like a native app.

Quick Capture — iOS Shortcut

Set this up once and you can send any copied text straight into Comms Queue in two taps from Slack, Mail, or Messages.

1
Open the Shortcuts app and tap + to create a new shortcut.
2
Tap Add Action, search for "Get Clipboard", and add it.
3

4
Tap the shortcut name at the top, rename it "Send to Queue", and tap Done.
5

Tips

Get the most out of Comms Queue.

Adding Tasks

→
Tap the label area of any bucket to add a new task directly into that bucket. The bucket will be pre-selected but you can change it.
→
The PASTE button reads your clipboard and drops it straight into the task field — great after copying a Slack message.

Editing Tasks

→
Tap the text of any task in the list to edit it — change the text, due date, bucket, notes, or source.
→
Tap the ☰ button on any task to move it to a different bucket or delete it.

Calendar View

→
Tasks without a due date appear as draggable chips at the top. Press and drag onto any day to set the due date — the bucket updates automatically.
→
Tap a chip to set a date or move it to a bucket without dragging.
→
Tap any day on the calendar to see everything due that day. From there you can complete, edit, or move any task.

General

→
The app works offline. Any changes you make will be saved and waiting when you reconnect.
→
When an update is available, a banner will appear at the top. Tap Update Now to get the latest version.
